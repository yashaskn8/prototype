# Milk Analyzer MA-100 Installation Guide

## Purpose
Customer-friendly installation and commissioning steps for the MA-100 milk analyzer.

## Before installation
- Put the analyzer on a stable, dry, level surface away from direct sun and vibration.
- Keep ventilation openings clear.
- Confirm the rated power supply on the machine nameplate.
- Keep the supplied sample cup, clean water, approved cleaning solution and lint-free wipes ready.
- Match model and serial details with the delivery note.

## Installation
1. Inspect the analyzer, cable, sampling tube and thermostat cup for transport damage.
2. Connect the sampling tube without bends or leaks.
3. Fit the thermostat cup fully in its holder.
4. Connect power only after accessories are fitted.
5. Switch on and wait for the startup self-check.
6. Run a clean-water cycle.
7. Run the approved verification sample.

## Escalate
Create a service call if the unit does not power on, leaks, repeatedly fails verification, or shows an undocumented error.
