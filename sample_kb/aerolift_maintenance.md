# Aerolift Preventive Maintenance Guide

## Daily operator check
Inspect the travel area, controls, emergency stop, access protection and visible components before use.

## Planned maintenance
A technician should review previous calls, recurring faults, replaced parts and project-specific notes before work. Record findings in the service report.

## Pre-visit parts planning
Use the complaint, exact model, previous failures and approved history to reserve likely consumables. Final diagnosis remains the technician's responsibility.
