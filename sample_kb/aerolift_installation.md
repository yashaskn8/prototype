# Aerolift-50mtr Installation and Handover Guide

## Site preparation
Confirm the location is level, accessible and clear of temporary obstructions. Verify project drawing, rated load, lifting height and power requirements.

## Installation
Only trained personnel may assemble lifting components. Confirm all supplied fasteners and safety devices are present. Electrical work must be done by authorised personnel.

## Commissioning
Test emergency stop, safety interlocks and unloaded travel before the approved commissioning test. Record project code, asset code, model, customer site and handover sign-off.

## Handover
Explain normal controls, emergency stop, daily visual checks, load restrictions and how to raise a service call.
