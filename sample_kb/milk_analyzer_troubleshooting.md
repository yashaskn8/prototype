# Milk Analyzer MA-100 Troubleshooting Guide

## Unstable reading
Check sample mixing, sample temperature, sampling tube connection, thermostat cup cleanliness and seating. Run a rinse and the approved verification sample. If verification remains outside the accepted range, stop testing and raise a service call.

## Does not draw sample
Check the sampling tube for bends or blockage and ensure the sample level is sufficient. Run the cleaning cycle. If the pump runs but no liquid moves, do not dismantle it; escalate to a technician.

## Power issue
Confirm the wall socket and approved power lead. If a known-good supply is present but the display stays off, create a service call.
