# Aerolift-50mtr Troubleshooting Guide

## Unit does not start
Confirm the main isolator is on, the emergency stop is released, and no safety interlock is open. Record any control-panel fault. Never bypass a safety interlock.

## Stops during travel
Check for a triggered limit or visible obstruction according to site procedure. If the issue repeats, isolate the equipment and raise a service call.

## Noise or vibration
Stop operation and record when the issue occurs and whether the unit is loaded. Do not continue if structural, drive or brake condition is uncertain.
