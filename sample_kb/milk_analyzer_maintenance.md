# Milk Analyzer MA-100 Preventive Maintenance Guide

## Daily
Complete the end-of-day cleaning cycle, inspect the sampling tube and record unusual errors.

## Weekly
Inspect the thermostat cup and sample path for deposits, inspect the power lead and review verification results for drift.

## Monthly
A trained technician reviews service history, tubing, temperature-control performance and calibration using the approved reference sample.

Every abnormal verification, part replacement and technician action should be stored against the asset so future RAG answers can use the service history.
